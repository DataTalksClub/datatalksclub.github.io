Jeanine Harb works as a Data Engineer at Ubisoft within the Data Office. Her work focuses on applying software engineering principles to data pipelines and improving the lifecycle of ML products through ML and DataOps practices, making sure machine learning systems are as well engineered and maintainable as the software around them. She also organizes data-related events at Ubisoft, helping grow the data community inside the company.

Before joining Ubisoft, Jeanine worked on a proof-of-concept for autonomous trains and contributed to open-sourcing a large-scale dataset for vision-based railway traffic light detection and recognition, combining computer vision and machine learning with real-world transportation safety problems.

Jeanine holds a diploma in Computer Engineering from Saint Joseph University of Beirut (Lebanon) and a Masters degree in Big Data and Machine Learning from Université Paris-Saclay, Télécom Paris, and Ecole Polytechnique (France). In her spare time, she is a trained facilitator for #IamRemarkable workshops, empowering others to talk about their achievements.
