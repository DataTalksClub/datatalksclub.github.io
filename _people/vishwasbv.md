Vishwas BV is a Data Scientist and Senior AI Consultant at Infosys, based in Bengaluru, India, where he helps enterprises apply machine learning to real business problems. His work draws on more than seven years of IT experience and covers data warehousing, big data, and data science, including machine learning, deep learning, time series analysis, natural language processing, reinforcement learning, and operations research.

His technical toolkit is broad: data migration and profiling, ETL and ELT pipelines, Python, PL/SQL, and Unix shell scripting, along with cloud platforms such as Azure ML Studio, Azure Cognitive Services, and AWS. This mix of engineering and data science experience allows him to take projects from raw data pipelines all the way to deployed intelligent applications.

Vishwas is also a co-author of Hands-on Time Series Analysis with Python, a practical book that walks readers through forecasting and time series techniques with real-world tools. He holds a Master of Technology in Software Engineering from the Birla Institute of Technology & Science, Pilani, and continues to focus on making advanced analytics dependable and production-ready.
