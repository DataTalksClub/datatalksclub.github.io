Pier Paolo Ippolito is a Data Scientist at SAS, one of the major names in analytics software. He holds an MSc in Artificial Intelligence and works at the intersection of data analytics, machine learning, and cloud development, turning research areas he cares about into applied solutions.

Beyond his day job, Pier Paolo is an active freelancer and a technical writer for Towards Data Science, one of the largest publications for data science and machine learning. Through his articles he breaks down topics across machine learning, data analytics, and cloud development for practitioners worldwide.

He also maintains a personal website and GitHub profile, where he shares code and resources with the data science community.
