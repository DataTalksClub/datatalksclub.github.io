Padma Chitturi works with Indeed as Senior Manager of Enterprise Data Analytics, where she focuses on building scalable data science platforms and enabling data-driven decision making across the business. Her work sits at the intersection of engineering and business: creating platforms that data scientists and analysts can build on, while keeping a deep passion for addressing real business needs with data.

Padma has extensive experience as a data engineer, a data scientist, and a data strategy consultant in previous roles. That blend of engineering depth and product skills shapes how she leads today: testing and building innovative data solutions in highly dynamic environments, always with a bias toward action.
