Serena Haidar is a Computer and Communications Engineer whose work sits at the intersection of connectivity, software, and applied artificial intelligence. While her formal training is in computer and communications engineering, she took her AI and machine learning skills into her own hands, steadily building them through online courses, tutorials, and projects of her own design. That self-directed path has given her a practical, end-to-end understanding of how technology is actually built, tested, and shipped.

Her toolkit spans Python, Java, Arduino, and other programming languages, and she is comfortable moving between software development and hardware prototyping. What sets Serena apart is the purpose behind the practice: she focuses on building technology that drives accessibility and sustainability, aiming to remove barriers for people and reduce the footprint of the systems we rely on.

A lifelong problem solver, Serena treats every hour of exploration as an investment. She keeps sharpening her skills and sharing what she learns, convinced that curiosity and consistent hands-on work are the most reliable way to grow as an engineer while making a positive impact.
