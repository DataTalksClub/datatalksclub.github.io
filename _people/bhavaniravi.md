Bhavani Ravi is a Software Engineer and AI Consultant who builds intelligent automation systems and data platforms. Working as a freelancer, she specializes in AI agents, workflow orchestration, and data pipelines, with deep hands-on experience across Apache Airflow, MCP, n8n, and Prefect. Her work sits where software engineering meets automation: helping teams replace manual processes with reliable, scalable systems.

Bhavani is an Apache Airflow Champion and a regular international tech speaker, sharing lessons on data engineering, orchestration, and open source. She is also a LinkedIn Learning instructor teaching Google Cloud data engineering and a vocal advocate for open-source education.

As the leader of TheLearningDev, a community for developers, she helps engineers deepen their expertise in data and AI through practical, accessible learning. Her combination of consulting work, teaching, and community building makes her a trusted voice for practitioners navigating the modern data and AI stack.
