Tony Zeljkovic is a hands-on AI and data platform consultant who specializes in the healthcare, life sciences, and insurance industries. He runs Narona Data, a consultancy through which he works directly with organizations to architect and deploy scalable, automated, cloud-native data and AI systems. His style is practical: he designs the architecture and then gets involved in building it, bridging data engineering, software development, machine learning, DevOps, and DataOps into platforms that actually ship.

Tony's background spans bioinformatics, clinical genomics, and large-scale data infrastructure, and that scientific foundation shows in the standards he holds his platforms to. In regulated industries, compliance is not optional, so he builds systems that meet HIPAA/HITECH and ACMG requirements while staying cost-efficient and tailored for growth. The outcomes his clients look for are enterprise-level: unlocking revenue, improving operational intelligence, and enabling innovation through applied AI.

Fluent in both technical and business domains, Tony collaborates with clients as a strategic partner rather than a vendor, helping them use AI-enabled infrastructure to sharpen their competitive edge. He is available for consulting engagements and committed to solving pressing business challenges with modern data and AI architecture.
