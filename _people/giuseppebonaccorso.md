Giuseppe Bonaccorso is an experienced data science manager whose expertise spans machine learning and deep learning, along with the organizational challenges of turning those technologies into business value. His career combines strong technical foundations with management experience, letting him work comfortably across model architecture, team leadership, and strategy.

His academic background reflects that dual profile. He earned his MSc Eng in Electronics Engineering in 2005 from the University of Catania, Italy, giving him a rigorous engineering grounding, and continued his studies with an MBA at the University of Rome Tor Vergata in Italy and the University of Essex in the UK, adding the business perspective needed to lead data initiatives end to end.

Giuseppe's main interests include machine learning and deep learning, data science strategy, and digital innovation in the healthcare industry, an area where analytical methods can have a direct impact on people's lives. He writes and shares his knowledge with the data science community, bridging the gap between technical practice and strategic decision making.
