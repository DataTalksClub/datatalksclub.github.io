Danny Ma describes himself as a recovering data scientist who has moved over to the dark side of ML and data engineering, and the label captures a career built on pragmatism. His core expertise spans data analytics, supervised machine learning algorithms, data architecture, and designing digital customer experiments for the retail and banking sectors. Over the years he has moved from SQL, SAS, and Excel workflows to Python, Kaggle projects, and production systems.

Danny's real passion is guiding businesses and individuals through their data and machine learning journeys. He runs the #DataWithDanny community, which has grown to more than 4,500 aspiring data professionals, and he shares career guidance like his ABC Framework for data science roles: Analyst, Builder, and Consultant. His advice blends honest transition tactics with the technical foundations teams actually use on the job.

He is now working toward his vision of a scalable virtual data apprenticeship program, aiming to spread practical, mentorship-driven data education well beyond a single classroom.
