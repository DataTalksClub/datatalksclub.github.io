Luke Whipps is a co-founder of Neural.AI, a company specializing in recruiting data scientists and other AI professionals. He has spent 8+ years recruiting in the data and analytics space, starting in traditional BI and analytics and evolving alongside the industry as advanced analytics, data science, machine learning, and deep learning became central to how companies hire.

Luke is also the host of the AI Game Changer podcast, where he talks with data professionals about careers, hiring, and the future of AI roles. His unusually balanced view of the hiring process, having sat on both sides of the table, makes his advice on standing out as a candidate and preparing for technical data science interviews especially practical. He has shared that perspective twice on the DataTalksClub podcast, covering both the recruitment process from the candidate's point of view and technical interview preparation.
