Sara Menefee is a product manager at Meroxa, a company building a data platform that helps software teams orchestrate and integrate data into their data-driven applications. Her path into data product management is anything but conventional: she started out as a product designer, working for companies including Sora, Checkr, Change.org, and Zendesk, before making a deliberate transition into product management for data products.

That design background shapes how Sara works today. She brings strong customer discovery and hypothesis formation skills to product decisions, and she is a firm believer that design thinking and close PM-designer collaboration lead to better prioritization and better products. On the technical side, she has invested in SQL, data engineering fundamentals, and the operational realities of managing data products, from data quality and PII compliance to the full data lifecycle that runs from sources through warehouses and into applications.

Sara is also passionate about helping other designers make the same leap. She advocates a transition strategy built on networking, on-the-job learning, mentorship, and case-study-style portfolios that clearly articulate the problem, the research, the solution, and the outcome.
