Illia Todor is a data engineer who builds the data platform at HRS. That work covers the foundations most teams only notice when they break: the pipelines, storage, and cloud infrastructure that turn raw data into something analysts and applications can rely on. As a certified AWS Cloud Practitioner, he works comfortably with cloud-native data tooling.

Illia sometimes contributes to open source and writes about data engineering and software design on [his Medium blog](https://iamtodor.medium.com/), sharing the practical lessons he picks up while building and maintaining data platforms.
