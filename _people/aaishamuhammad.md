Aaisha Muhammad is a self-taught bioinformatician, machine learning engineer, and data scientist based in Johannesburg, South Africa. Without a traditional academic route into the field, she built her skills through open curricula, deliberate practice, and a steady stream of hands-on projects, and she is a graduate of the DataTalks.Club Machine Learning Zoomcamp.

Her project work follows a dataset-first approach: sourcing datasets and papers through tools like Google Scholar and PubMed, then building capstone projects such as frog toxicity and landscape classifiers. She also works with deployment and MLOps fundamentals, including Docker and Kubernetes, to move her models beyond notebooks and into working systems.

Aaisha writes about bioinformatics and machine learning on her blog, sharing practical study tactics and resource recommendations aimed at other self-taught learners. Beyond data work, her interests extend to scientific illustration, photography, and digital art.
