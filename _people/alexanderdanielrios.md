Alexander has been a Python developer for over 7 years, and for the past 3 years he has focused on applying data science techniques to real-world challenges. That combination of solid software engineering habits and applied machine learning training shapes how he approaches problems: start with the data, keep the solution practical, and let measurable results drive decisions.

He is now transitioning into a role as a Data Scientist or Machine Learning Engineer, bringing with him the discipline of an experienced developer and the curiosity of someone who genuinely enjoys turning data into answers.
