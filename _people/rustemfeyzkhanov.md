Rustem Feyzkhanov is an AWS Machine Learning Hero, a recognition of his expertise and contributions to the machine learning community on AWS. His work centers on a question that every organization scaling AI eventually faces: how to use cloud infrastructure to train, deploy, and serve deep learning models efficiently and affordably.

He has hands-on experience architecting and deploying deep learning training and inference pipelines, covering the full journey from experimentation to production workloads. Rustem is passionate about the use of cloud infrastructure for AI and ML applications, and he approaches it with a builder's mindset, favoring open tools and reproducible setups.

Rustem shares his work openly with the community through open-source projects on GitHub and through his website, ryfeus.io, where he publishes practical resources on cloud-based machine learning for engineers who want to run real workloads rather than just read about them.
