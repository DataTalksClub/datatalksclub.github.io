Gonçalo Sequeira is the founder of Hiire, the recruiting company he opened in 2022 to help companies hire better and faster every single day without forgetting an amazing candidate experience. He built Hiire as a 100% digital business so he could run it while traveling the world, expanding its reach to international companies.

His recruiting background includes working for companies like Feedzai, Mercedes-Benz.io, Adyen, and Trengo. Over that time he hired more than 500 IT professionals, from software engineers to CTOs, and coached recruitment teams to reach their next level across Portugal, Germany, the Netherlands, and Singapore.

Since 2020, Gonçalo has also been actively helping great professionals find better jobs and different career paths through personalized coaching programs. That combination of hands-on hiring experience and one-on-one coaching makes him a practical guide for anyone navigating the tech job market, whether they are a candidate or a company trying to build a stronger team.
