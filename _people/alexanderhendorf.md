Alexander Hendorf is an independent AI strategy consultant who specializes in regulated industries, including finance, insurance, and critical infrastructure. Through his consultancy opotoc GmbH, he advises organizations on building durable AI foundations instead of patchwork implementations, combining data-first architecture with pragmatic MLOps practices such as standardization, CI/CD, governance, and reproducibility. His work centers on helping enterprises move from proof of concept to production: aligning AI initiatives with business goals, running realistic experiments, and setting up retraining pipelines and feedback loops that keep models useful over time.

Beyond client work, Alexander is a leading figure in the international Python and data community. He heads PyCon DE and PyData, Europe's leading conference for applied AI and open source, and initiated Pioneers Hub. He is a Python Software Foundation Fellow and a EuroPython Fellow, serves on the board of the Python Software Verband, and founded the AG Open Source within Germany's KI Bundesverband in 2026.

With more than a decade of enterprise consulting experience and over 100 conference appearances, Alexander is a sought-after speaker and advisor on the intersection of AI strategy, open source sovereignty, and practical deployment in high-compliance environments.
