Nick Singh is best known as the co-author, with Kevin Huo, of Ace the Data Science Interview, a book built on real interview questions from data science and analytics hiring, along with practical advice on SQL, machine learning, probability, and behavioral interviews. Through the book and his career coaching, he has helped thousands of candidates prepare for data roles and land jobs in tech.

Nick's own career gives him credibility on both sides of the interview table. He started out as a Software Engineer on Facebook's Growth Team, worked at SafeGraph, a location analytics startup, and in college interned at Microsoft and on the Data Infrastructure team at Google's Nest Labs. He graduated from the University of Virginia with a degree in Systems Engineering and a minor in Computer Science and Applied Math.

As an educator and career coach, Nick focuses on the non-technical skills that candidates often neglect: behavioral interviews told through STAR stories, building an impact-driven portfolio, case interview strategy, and even writing cold emails that get responses. His mix of Big Tech experience and practical coaching makes him a go-to resource for anyone breaking into data science, data engineering, and analytics.
