This work required balancing technical modernization with strict data governance, and it established her reputation for reliable, compliant, and well-documented IT practices.

Ella's technology journey began with early experiments in coding and has evolved alongside modern AI tools such as ChatGPT, which she uses to boost productivity while staying grounded in ethics and empathy. She is passionate about the potential of artificial intelligence in healthcare, education, and climate action, and she advocates for responsible data use that genuinely enhances people's lives.
