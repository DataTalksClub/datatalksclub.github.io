Krzysztof (Kris) Ograbek is a content creator, lifelong learner, and a member of the 5 A.M. Club, whose discipline shows in the consistency of his work. In a three-year-long search for a perfect niche, he created content about a wide range of topics, such as blockchain, fatherhood, and learning how to learn, an exploration that taught him how to explain complex subjects to any audience.

His current, and hopefully final, passion is building practical projects based on Large Language Models. As a believer in the teach everything you learn motto, Kris documents what he builds and breaks on three platforms: YouTube, Medium, and LinkedIn. Instead of keeping knowledge to himself, he turns his experiments with LLMs into step-by-step lessons that others can follow and reuse in their own projects.

After work, Kris turns into a world-class dad and husband, a role he treats with the same curiosity and dedication he brings to learning in public.
