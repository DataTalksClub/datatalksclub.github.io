Loris Marini is the CEO and founder of Discovering Data, on a mission to build a bridge between business leaders and data leaders. Through his company and his show he helps organizations close the communication gap that keeps data investments from turning into business results, focusing on alignment, storytelling, and the practical skills data professionals need to create impact.

Loris hosts the Discovering Data podcast, a show for business leaders and data professionals who want to turn data into outcomes. In long-form conversations he explores the mindset, skills, and strategies of successful leaders, and covers topics such as deploying machine learning in SaaS businesses, defining metrics that matter, and presenting data insights as stories that persuade stakeholders.

His work sits at the intersection of data science, leadership, and communication, and he brings a practitioner's empathy to the everyday challenges data teams face when translating models and metrics into decisions that executives can act on.
