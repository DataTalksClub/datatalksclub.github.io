Manmohan Gosada is a seasoned data science professional with a comprehensive background spanning various data science functions and industries. He has successfully led large-scale data science projects, leveraging cutting-edge technologies to implement transformative products, and has emerged as a leader in driving innovation and delivering impactful solutions that hold up in production, not just in notebooks.

Manmohan is a co-author of Data-Centric Machine Learning with Python (Packt), alongside Nakul Bajaj and Jonas Christensen. The book reflects his practical focus on the data side of machine learning: annotation quality, data drift, and the monitoring and evaluation practices that determine whether models keep working after deployment.

With a postgraduate degree grounding him in the theoretical foundations of data science, Manmohan is equally passionate about sharing what he has learned. A captivating speaker, he engages audiences with a blend of expertise and enthusiasm, demystifying complex concepts and helping practitioners at every level grow in the world of data science.
