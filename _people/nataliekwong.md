Natalie Kwong is a Growth Product Manager at Airbyte, where she focuses on building the user experience and overseeing analytics for the data integration platform. Her expertise is in scaling analytics teams and systems from the ground up, turning messy early-stage data into dependable infrastructure for decision making.

Before Airbyte, Natalie held analytics and operations roles at Harness, KeepTruckin, and AppDynamics. That hands-on background shapes how she thinks about data tooling: ETL versus ELT, data lakes versus warehouses, change data capture, orchestration, and the governance practices that keep data lakes from turning into data swamps. She has shared these perspectives with the community, including a DataTalks.Club conversation on making sense of data engineering acronyms and buzzwords.

Natalie currently resides in New York. Outside of work, you can find her creating pottery, plant shopping, or playing recreational dodgeball.
