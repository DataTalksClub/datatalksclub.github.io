Rishabh Bhargava has spent more than 7 years working with analytics and machine learning teams. Most recently, he led Sales Engineering at Datacoral, a data infrastructure company acquired by Cloudera, where he helped analytics teams build and run their data pipelines. Before that, he was an early employee at Primer.ai, building and deploying machine learning models for multiple natural language applications such as summarization and entity extraction.

His expertise sits at the intersection of data infrastructure and production ML: model APIs and SLAs, A/B testing and shadow deployments, the evolution of fraud detection from rule-based systems to machine learning, and how to structure data teams that actually ship. He shares these perspectives through talks and podcast conversations on MLOps, experiments, and data team building.

Rishabh also writes [MLOps Roundup](https://mlopsroundup.substack.com/), a newsletter that digs into the practical challenges of running machine learning in production. He holds a Master's in Computer Science from Stanford and completed his undergraduate studies at the University of Cambridge.
