Vin Vashishta works in applied machine learning, but his attention is fixed on the question that decides whether ML efforts survive: how does the technology make money? As an Engineer Strategist, he has built and brought machine learning products to market with ARR in the hundreds of millions of dollars, and he advises organizations on the strategy, structure, and execution needed to get models from prototype to profit.

His current focus is monetizing machine learning, which covers revenue and pricing strategies, model reliability, and defining and hiring for the roles that make monetization possible, including ML Researcher, ML Architect, and ML Product Manager. Just as important is the path to production: the engineering discipline that turns a promising model into a dependable product, because revenue only counts after the model ships and keeps working.

Vin shares this playbook publicly. At the DataTalks.Club conference he presented New Roles and Key Skills to Monetize Machine Learning, laying out the capabilities teams need to move models into the revenue column, and he has appeared on the DataTalks.Club podcast to discuss converting ML models into ARR and MRR with product and MLOps strategy. Through his advisory work and writing, he helps leadership teams treat machine learning as a business investment rather than a cost center.
