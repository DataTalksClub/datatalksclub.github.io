Dave Flynn is a Technical Advocate at InfuseAI, the company behind open-source projects such as PrimeHub, ArtiVC, and PipeRider. In his role, Dave focuses on technical writing, documentation, and marketing, turning the output of engineering teams into clear material that helps developers evaluate, install, and get value from open-source tools. The position puts him at the meeting point of software engineering, open-source community building, and technical communication.

Before joining InfuseAI, Dave worked as a full-stack web developer and as a Linux system administrator. That background matters for how he does his job: he writes for developers from firsthand experience with the systems, deployments, and day-to-day frustrations their tooling needs to solve. Dave's mix of engineering practice and communication skills makes him a practical, credible guide for teams navigating the open-source tooling landscape.
