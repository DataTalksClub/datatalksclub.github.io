Admond Lee Kin Lim is currently a Data Science Instructor at Hackwagon Academy, with a mission to make data science accessible to everyone through education. Alongside teaching, he works as an independent data scientist and consultant, helping startups with various data science and machine learning projects, from first prototypes to production-ready solutions.

Prior to joining Hackwagon Academy, Admond was a Data Scientist at Micron, focusing on Smart Manufacturing and AI, where he applied machine learning to large-scale manufacturing problems. This combination of corporate scale and startup agility gives him a practical, well-rounded view of what it takes to deliver value with data.

Admond is also a passionate data science communicator. His work and experience have been featured in publications including KDnuggets, Medium, Tech in Asia, and AI Time Journal, and he has spoken at events such as the Data Science UA Conference 2020 and DataTalksClub, where he shared a practical playbook for building a personal brand as a data professional, from publishing on LinkedIn and Medium to monetizing expertise with online courses.
