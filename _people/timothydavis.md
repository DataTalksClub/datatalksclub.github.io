Tim Davis is a Developer Advocate with years of experience in infrastructure, operations, DevOps, and data. That background means he does not just explain technology from the outside: he has lived the on-call shifts, the pipelines, and the production incidents that shape how tools are actually used by engineering teams.

As an advocate, Tim is as comfortable on a large global conference stage as he is in a small community meetup, and he has built a plethora of written and recorded content online across numerous niches of the tech industry. His work focuses on meeting practitioners where they are, turning hard-won operational experience into content that helps engineers learn faster and avoid predictable mistakes.
