Louis is an AI engineer at SandboxAQ, the parent company behind Switch. He and his team built Switch to solve a problem they experienced firsthand: as they adopted more AI agents across different tools and frameworks, putting those agents to work across a team required new integrations and repeated context sharing. Switch grew out of that need, bringing people and agents together in the collaboration tools teams already use while keeping context and history with the work.
