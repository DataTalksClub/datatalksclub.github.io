Hrishi Olickel is the co-founder and CEO of Southbridge AI, where he builds agentic systems and open-source infrastructure for turning messy, real-world enterprise data into reliable, repeatable pipelines.

He has worked on data and verification problems for a decade. In undergrad he co-authored Oyente (ACM CCS 2016), the symbolic-execution analyzer for Ethereum smart contracts whose code joined the Ethereum Foundation. Before Southbridge he spent five years as co-founder and CTO of Greywing (YC W21), building operational data systems for the maritime industry and one of the earliest agentic retrieval systems, WalkingRAG.

He is the creator of the open-source Hankweave runtime and is known for his long-form technical essays and talks on prompting, evaluations, and agentic data engineering.
