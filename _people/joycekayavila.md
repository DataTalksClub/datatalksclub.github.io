Joyce Kay Avila is a data and AI strategist who has spent more than 25 years as a business and technology leader. Her background bridges the technical and the commercial: she holds bachelor's and master's degrees in computer science and business administration, and completed her PhD coursework in accounting information systems, a combination that lets her speak fluently to both engineers and executives about how data platforms create value.

Joyce's platform expertise is deep and formally recognized. She holds numerous Salesforce and Slack certifications, along with Tableau certification, and is certified in Snowflake. She has been named a Snowflake Data Superhero every year since that MVP program began in 2019, a recognition of her standing in the Snowflake community.

A committed educator, Joyce produces a running series of how-to videos for the Salesforce and Snowflake communities on her YouTube channels. She is also the author of several O'Reilly books, including Snowflake: The Definitive Guide in its first and second editions, Hands-on Salesforce Data Cloud, and Salesforce AI, books that have become go-to resources for teams architecting, designing, and deploying modern data and AI platforms.
