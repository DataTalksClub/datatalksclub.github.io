Greg Coquillo builds AI products that make a measurable difference at enterprise scale. As a Technology Manager at Amazon, he owns a roadmap that drives the adoption of AI products supporting Private Brands' Product Safety and Compliance, working with data scientists, software engineers, and business intelligence engineers to leverage machine learning and big data analytics to design predictive models that enhance business decision-making and performance.

His approach pairs machine learning with practical product thinking: working backwards from business problems, mapping the customer journey, and defining success metrics such as pipeline reliability, SLAs, and data quality. The outcomes of this work help accelerate growth, reduce time to market for products, monitor cross-geo compliance and import requirements, and ensure customer safety.

Greg's passion for solving concrete business use cases, such as price optimization for commodity-based products, is what first led him to deep dive into the world of artificial intelligence. He now shares lessons on building and scaling AI data products, covering MLOps roadmaps, customer research, and product management for data teams.
