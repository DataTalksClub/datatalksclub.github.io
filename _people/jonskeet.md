Jon Skeet is a staff developer relations engineer at Google, where he currently works on the Google Cloud Client Libraries for .NET, helping developers build cloud applications on Google Cloud Platform. His work sits at the meeting point of developer tooling, cloud computing, and the .NET ecosystem, and his ability to explain subtle technical details has made him a trusted voice for C# developers worldwide.

Jon is the author of C# in Depth, published by Manning and currently in its fourth edition, a book widely regarded as essential reading for serious C# developers. He also co-authored Software Mistakes and Tradeoffs with Tomasz Lelek, a book about the everyday decisions and trade-offs that shape reliable software. Beyond writing, he created NodaTime, an alternative date and time library for .NET that reflects his close attention to the correctness problems hiding in ordinary code.

He is perhaps best known for his contributions to Stack Overflow, where his clear and precise answers to programming questions have helped millions of developers and made his name closely associated with the community itself.
