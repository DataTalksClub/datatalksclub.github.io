Haziqa Sajid is a data scientist and developer advocate who works at the intersection of AI, machine learning, and data infrastructure. She has built a reputation for in-depth technical content covering some of the most active areas in applied AI, including large language models, vector databases, RAG systems, MLOps, and computer vision. Her writing helps engineers and product teams understand not just how these systems work, but how to put them into practice.

As the founder of Disruptivera, Haziqa leads distributed teams that deliver whitepapers, case studies, developer documentation, and data-driven marketing for global SaaS and AI companies. This combination of hands-on data science experience and technical communication allows her to translate complex systems into clear, practical resources that developers can actually use.

Whether she is explaining retrieval-augmented generation, evaluating vector databases, or walking through MLOps workflows, Haziqa brings a practitioner's perspective to developer education and makes advanced AI and data infrastructure topics accessible to a broad technical audience.
