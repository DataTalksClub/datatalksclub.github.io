Valeriia Kuka is a Content Manager at DataTalks.Club, one of the largest global communities for data practitioners, where she shapes educational content on artificial intelligence, machine learning, and data science. Her work centers on turning complex technical topics into clear, practical explainers that help professionals at every level keep up with a fast-moving field.

She has built an AI-focused audience of more than 60,000 people, earning reposts from organizations such as Stanford NLP, Amazon Research, and Hugging Face. Her background also includes collaborating with AI and ML newsletters and global communities that reach over 100,000 members, giving her a broad view of what resonates with practitioners, researchers, and learners alike.

Beyond trend coverage, Valeriia writes concise explainers and historical pieces that connect today's breakthroughs in AI and machine learning to the ideas that shaped them. Through articles, community programs, and podcast promotions for DataTalks.Club, she supports the learning journeys of thousands of data scientists, engineers, and analysts around the world.
