Alicja Notowska is a seasoned talent acquisition specialist, or as she jokingly calls herself, a professional stalker. She has more than 10 years of tech recruitment experience gained at some of the most competitive employers in the European tech scene, including Google and Zalando, and most recently at WeAreKeen, an embedded talent agency that works inside the companies it hires for.

Her recruiting focus is on data roles: she regularly advises on hiring data scientists and analysts, covering the full lifecycle from talent pipelines and job specifications to CV screening, interviews, and salary expectations. On the DataTalks.Club podcast she has shared practical guidance on what hiring managers actually look for and how candidates can stand out in a crowded market.

That combination of in-house and agency experience, across both big tech and startups, gives Alicja an unusually complete view of the tech hiring process from the recruiter's side of the table.
