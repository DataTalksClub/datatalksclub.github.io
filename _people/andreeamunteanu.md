Andreea Munteanu is a Product Manager at Canonical, where she focuses on Machine Learning Operations. In this role she works at the intersection of open source infrastructure and applied AI, shaping the tools that help organizations run machine learning reliably at scale.

Her perspective is grounded in hands-on data science experience across a range of industries. She has applied AI techniques to help enterprises get real value from their initiatives and make data-driven decisions, collaborating on projects in retail, telco, and the shared economy. Across these engagements her work concentrated on understanding customer behavior patterns, improving cost efficiency, and building solutions that scale.

That combination of practitioner experience and product leadership makes Andreea effective at bridging the gap between what data science teams need and what MLOps platforms deliver, and a clear voice on how enterprises can turn machine learning projects into production systems.
