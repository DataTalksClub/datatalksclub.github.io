Jonas Christensen has spent his career leading data science functions across multiple industries, building and running the teams that turn data and machine learning into business impact. That experience on the front line of data leadership shapes everything he does today, from teaching to advising organizations on their analytics strategies.

Jonas is an international keynote speaker on data science and analytics leadership and a postgraduate educator and advisor in the field of data science and machine learning. He is the host of the Leaders of Analytics podcast, where he interviews practitioners and leaders about what it really takes to build successful data science teams and careers.

He is also a co-author of Data-Centric Machine Learning with Python (Packt), a practical book on improving the quality of the data that powers machine learning systems. Jonas is passionate about what data science and AI can do for the world of business and beyond, and he believes data science and AI will be as revolutionary to the way we do business and interact with each other as IT and personal computing have been over the past 40 years.
