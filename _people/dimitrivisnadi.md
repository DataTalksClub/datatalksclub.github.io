Dimitri Visnadi is an independent data consultant with a focus on data strategy. He has consulted for companies that lead the marketing data space, including Unilever, Ferrero, Heineken, and Red Bull, and has been part of data departments at Hewlett-Packard (HP) and a Google-partnered consulting firm, where he worked on data products and strategy. He has lived and worked in six countries across Europe, in both corporate and startup organizations.

Dimitri holds a Master's in Business Analytics with Computer Science from University College London and a Bachelor in Business Administration from John Cabot University. He keeps close ties to academia and holds a mentor position in entrepreneurship at both institutions.

Through his platform, The Data Freelancer, Dimitri publishes a newsletter and practical guidance on building a sustainable independent career in data, covering pricing, client acquisition, contracts, and market positioning. He draws on his own move from corporate analytics into freelancing and regularly speaks about freelancer business models, in-demand data skills, and how AI tools are changing the way independent consultants work.
