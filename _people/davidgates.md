David Gates is the founder of [Accents Welcome](https://accentswelcome.com), an English language school built specifically for data professionals. His coaching focuses on the communication skills that technical careers depend on: explaining models and analyses to non-technical stakeholders, presenting with confidence, and collaborating across cultures and accents.

David works with data scientists, analysts, engineers, and other specialists who need their expertise to be understood. By combining language teaching with the practical realities of working in data, he helps professionals make sure their ideas get heard and their work gets the credit it deserves.
