Ilya Boytsov is a deep learning research engineer focused on generative AI. At Wayfair, he works on GenAI projects, applying modern large language model techniques to real e-commerce problems.

Ilya is also deeply engaged in the LLM community. He co-leads the Oxford LLMs project at the University of Oxford and runs the Street Smart AI meetup in Berlin, Germany, creating spaces where researchers and practitioners share practical experience with building and deploying large language models.
