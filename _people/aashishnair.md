​Aashish Nair is a Data Engineer at dltHub, where he works on dlt, an open source tool for data ingestion. He is the creator of the popular dlt deployment course, which teaches data teams how to run dlt pipelines in production rather than leaving them on a laptop, and his material focuses on the deployment practices that keep pipelines dependable once they are live.

Through his course and his work at dltHub, Aashish helps engineers get more value from open source data tooling, with a particular eye toward ELT workflows and production readiness.
