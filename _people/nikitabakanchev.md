Nik Bakanchev is a hands-on Data Engineer and Platform Architect who specializes in enterprise-scale data infrastructure. His work centers on the platforms organizations rely on to move, store, and process data reliably at scale, and he is known for a practitioner's approach to architecture: patterns proven in production rather than in theory.

His current focus lies at the intersection of data engineering and AI, specifically designing, observing, and securing autonomous agentic workflows in production. This puts him on the front line of a shift many data teams are now facing, as AI agents move from demos into business-critical pipelines and platforms require new approaches to observability, security, and reliability.

Nik shares practical patterns and field observations on the future of data platforms as an independent industry expert, offering data engineers and architects a grounded view of how enterprise data infrastructure is evolving in the age of agentic AI.
