Ashish Patel is a Senior Data Scientist, AI researcher, and AI consultant with over seven years of experience in the field of AI. He currently works as a Senior Data Scientist at Cynet infotech Pvt Ltd and lives in Ahmedabad, India, where he has built a career on cross-domain projects involving a wide variety of data, platforms, and technologies.

His research footprint is substantial for a practitioner. Ashish holds a Master of Engineering degree from Gujarat Technological University and has published more than 15 research papers in the field of data science with reputed publications such as IEEE. His research interests and ambitions span a broad set of domains, including machine learning, deep learning, time series, natural language processing, reinforcement learning, audio analytics, signal processing, sensor technology, IoT, and computer vision, a range that explains his comfort moving between very different problem types.

Ashish is also a proven competitor. He holds Rank 3 as a Kernel Master on Kaggle, one of the most demanding public benchmarks of practical data science skill. Combined with his consulting experience and publication record, that makes him a versatile voice on applied AI, equally credible discussing research directions and the realities of shipping models across industries.
