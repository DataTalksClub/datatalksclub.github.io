Kate Ogochukwu Nwankwo is the co-founder of Datatera, a company built on the belief that AI-powered technology and data can drive real business growth. Alongside her startup work, she serves as a Data Science and AI Engineering fellow at Women Techsters, an initiative that helps women develop the skills to build careers in technology.

Her work bridges practice and mentorship: applying data science and AI engineering to business problems while opening doors for the next generation of data professionals.
