Orlando Hohmeier has been developing complex, highly scalable software products for more than 17 years, working across a wide range of industries from automotive to telecommunications. Over that time he has become a recognized full-stack expert for distributed systems, the kind of architecture work that sits behind reliable, high-performance products where scalability and correctness both matter.

As an engineering leader, Orlando is responsible for the engineering team and places a strong emphasis on excellent solutions and disciplined engineering practice. A recurring theme in his work is making AI genuinely applicable in manufacturing, connecting modern machine learning capabilities with the demanding requirements of industrial environments.
