Philip Christos is an Associate at M2VC based in Barcelona, Spain, where he focuses on investing in frontier AI. His work centers on understanding where AI capability is heading, from agents and model performance to the scaling and retrieval infrastructure that the next generation of AI products will be built on.

Before joining M2VC in 2026, Philip spent four years at Terra VC, rising from Analyst to Associate. That experience across both firms has given him a broad view of the startup landscape and of what separates durable AI companies from short-lived hype.

Beyond investing, Philip is an active voice in the tech community. He frequently shares insights on AI agents, model capabilities, and the evolving AI infrastructure landscape, including through his own channel, making him a useful connector for founders and operators navigating the fast-moving AI ecosystem.
