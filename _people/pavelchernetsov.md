Pavel Chernetsov grew up in the United States and has built his career with words: he has worked as a professional translator and copywriter for over six years. Today he lives near the Black Sea in the mountains of Georgia with his wife, working across languages and formats for a range of clients.

An avid reader, Pavel especially enjoys reading and writing about all topics related to data science, machine learning, and coding. Away from the desk, he likes to ride jet-skis and is known for making a mean shish kabob.
