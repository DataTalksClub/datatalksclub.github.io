Having joined Kaggle more than 10 years ago, Luca Massaron is a Kaggle Grandmaster in discussions and a Kaggle Master in both competitions and notebooks. In Kaggle competitions he climbed as high as no. 7 in the worldwide rankings, an achievement that reflects years of hands-on work with machine learning, ensembles, and creative feature engineering under competitive pressure.

On the professional side, Luca is a data scientist with over a decade of experience in transforming data into smarter artifacts, solving real-world problems, and generating value for businesses and stakeholders. He is a Google Developer Expert (GDE) in machine learning, and he regularly shares his expertise with the data science community through books, discussions, and mentoring.

Luca is the author of best-selling books on AI, machine learning, and algorithms. His bibliography includes The Kaggle Book (co-authored with Konrad Banachewicz), Machine Learning Using TensorFlow Cookbook (co-authored with Alexia Audevart and Konrad Banachewicz), and Machine Learning for Tabular Data (co-authored with Mark Ryan), works that help practitioners go from fundamentals to competition-grade and production-grade machine learning.
