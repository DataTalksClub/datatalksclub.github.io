Lera backs her product decisions with data. She is skilled in Python, SQL, Google Sheets, and Power BI, which she uses to analyze data and track performance metrics, and she is familiar with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Figma, allowing her to contribute across the full range of e-commerce projects.
