Sebastian Ayala Ruano is a bioinformatics software developer working at the seam between biotechnology and computational biology. He builds open-source software that makes complex biological data analysis accessible to researchers, with contributions to tools including MicW2Graph, VueGen, and VueCore, projects designed to simplify multi-omics data analysis so scientists can spend less time on plumbing and more time on science.

His background covers a wide range of computational biology applications. Sebastian has worked on cheminformatics and peptide discovery, applied network-based analysis to biological questions, and developed educational bioinformatics tools for open science communities. He has also shared practical experience building reproducible, scalable bioinformatics workflows for sequencing and metagenomics with open-source tools on the DataTalks.Club podcast.

Sebastian is currently pursuing a Master's degree in Systems Biology at Maastricht University, where he focuses on integrating machine learning and network science into biological research. That combination, formal training in systems biology plus hands-on software engineering, shapes his goal of turning cutting-edge computational methods into dependable, reusable tools for the life sciences. He shares his projects and insights through his personal website and GitHub.
