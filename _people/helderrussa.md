Hélder Russa is a Data Engineering Lead at Jumia, where he contributes to the strategy definition, design, and implementation of multiple Jumia data platforms. He has over 10 years of professional experience in computer science, with a background in Information Technologies and Data Science and a lasting focus on evolving and maintaining data solutions applied to decision making. He leads the engineering work that turns raw data into reliable, well-modeled datasets for analytics across the business.

Since 2018, Hélder has also been a co-founder and data architect of ShopAI, a company specialized in deep learning that leverages the capabilities of images to optimize search channels inside webshops, bringing research-grade computer vision into production e-commerce.

Hélder is a co-author of Analytics Engineering with SQL and dbt, an O'Reilly book written with Rui Machado that shows data teams how to build meaningful analytics layers with modern tooling. His combination of platform leadership, entrepreneurship, and hands-on data engineering makes him a practical voice on building data infrastructure that organizations can trust.
