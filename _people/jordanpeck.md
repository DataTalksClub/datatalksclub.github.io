Jordan Peck is Director of Field Engineering at Snowplow, where he works with the company's most complex and strategic customers to solve behavioral data challenges. His role bridges engineering and data teams: he helps them get Snowplow pipelines into production and extract value from event streams to power real-time analytics and agentic AI use cases.

Working at the intersection of event data infrastructure and customer outcomes, Jordan brings deep practical knowledge of what it takes to run behavioral data in production, from tracking design to scaling event streams for machine learning and real-time applications. He also writes about data and engineering on Medium, sharing lessons from his work with teams building on behavioral data.
