Alena Astrakhantseva works at the intersection of engineering, data science, and community. At dltHub, the company behind dlt, the open source data load tool, she wears several hats: Python developer, data scientist, and developer relations professional. Her technical work centers on building and optimizing data pipelines, spanning both ELT and ETL patterns, with an eye for how data moves through real, messy systems.

Just as important is the human side of her role. Alena engages with the dlt community, helping users get the most out of the tool, and creates educational content that makes data processing more approachable. That combination of hands-on pipeline engineering and clear teaching makes her a useful guide for anyone working to get data from where it lives into a state where it can actually be used.
