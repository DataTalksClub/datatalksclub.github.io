Fabiana Clemente is a data scientist whose background ranges from business intelligence to big data development and IoT architecture. Throughout her career she has led state-of-the-art projects in both global companies and startups, developing the blend of engineering depth and business awareness that defines her work today.

As YData's Co-Founder and Chief Data Officer, Fabiana combines data privacy with deep learning as her main fields of work and research, all in service of one mission: improving data quality. She is a frequent speaker on why better data is the foundation of better machine learning, including a talk on the importance of data quality at the DataTalksClub Summer Marathon.

Fabiana has an academic background in Applied Maths and an MSc in Data Management, combined with nano degrees in Deep Learning and Secure and Private AI. That mix of mathematics, engineering, and privacy expertise underpins her approach to making high-quality data accessible to every data science team.
