Anusha Akkina is the co-founder of Aurelytix, an AI-driven finance platform built to give CFOs and finance teams clarity and speed without adding complexity. In 2025 she launched the company's flagship product, Insight Pulse, an AI-powered decision layer that spots anomalies, explains variances, and surfaces opportunities while keeping a human firmly in the loop.

Her perspective comes from the inside of enterprise finance. Anusha is an alumna of CIMA, UK, and after almost a decade in corporate finance strategy and transformation, leading ERP implementations and major finance change programs at companies like Deloitte, Parexel, Survitec, and Chubb, she grew frustrated with data silos and slow, fragmented tools that keep enterprises and SMEs reactive instead of strategic. Aurelytix is her answer to that frustration: finance technology designed around how finance teams actually work.

Anusha is passionate about augmented finance, using AI to supercharge decision-making rather than replace judgment. That conviction shapes how she builds: pairing advanced analytics with explainability, compliance, and trust, especially under GDPR in Europe. For CFOs weighing AI adoption against regulatory reality, she offers a rare combination of Chartered Institute of Management Accountants training, enterprise transformation experience, and hands-on AI product building.
