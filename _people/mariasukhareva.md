Maria Sukhareva is a principal key expert in Artificial Intelligence at Siemens, with over 15 years of experience at the forefront of generative AI technologies. Her path into industry AI is distinctive: she began as a linguist and moved into computational AI, and that combination of language expertise and engineering depth still shapes how she approaches natural language systems today.

At Siemens, Maria is known for her keen eye for technological innovation and for transforming cutting-edge AI research into practical, value-driven tools that address real-world needs. Her work is hands-on and results focused: she has led large-scale chatbot security exercises, exposing failure modes such as prompt injection and knowledge-base exfiltration, and she designs defenses that hold up in production, including output validation, query analysis, layered defenses, and non-LLM classifiers. She also works on human-in-the-loop moderation workflows and prompt customization for controlled machine translation.

Across all of her projects, Maria pursues a balanced vision in which the power of innovation is matched by ethical responsibility. Her commitment to scalable, long-term solutions has made her a trusted voice on how enterprises can deploy generative AI systems that are accurate, secure, and genuinely useful.
