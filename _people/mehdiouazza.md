Mehdi Ouazza is a data geek and entrepreneur with more than seven years of experience working across nearly every aspect of data engineering: streaming and batch pipelines, data modeling, orchestration, infrastructure, and, from time to time, analytical reporting with dashboarding tools. His career has run from business intelligence and on-premise big data environments to staff-level data engineering, giving him a ground-level view of how data platforms evolve as companies grow.

That experience shapes how he thinks about scale: building self-service data platforms that let teams move without bottlenecks, adopting event streaming with Kafka, schema registries, and data contracts, and hiring senior engineers who can raise the bar. He is equally interested in the human side of the craft, from onboarding and junior learning paths to the culture shifts that hypergrowth demands.

A self-described continuous learner, Mehdi keeps a regular technology watch and shares what he picks up along the way as a startup entrepreneur, meetup regular, and blogger. His motto: sharing is caring.
