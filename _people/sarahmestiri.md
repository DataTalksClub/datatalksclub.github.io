Sarah Mestiri is a Data Scientist and a Certified Career and Interview Coach with more than six years of experience in tech. She has worked at international companies, financial companies such as FIS, and startups, an environment-hopping career that has given her a clear view of what data roles look like across organizations of very different shapes and sizes.

Sarah's own experience of going through a career transition, combined with a natural drive to support other women in following their professional dreams, inspired her to become a certified career coach. She is on a mission to help more women find the right job for them and achieve their full potential.

As a coach, Sarah works with women returning to work after a career break and with professionals transitioning into the data field. Her coaching method is practical and structured: define your ideal role, validate your skills with portfolio projects, build a CV that tells a coherent story, and run networking as a weekly practice with personalized outreach rather than a one-off activity. She shares this framework through talks, workshops, and community programs.
