Alexia Audevart is a data scientist and the founder of datactik, where she helps clients solve business problems by making their applications smarter. Her work focuses on applying machine learning and artificial intelligence in practical, business-driven ways, turning models into features that genuinely improve products and user experiences.

Alexia is recognized as a Google Developer Expert in machine learning, a distinction awarded to professionals with demonstrated expertise and a track record of sharing knowledge with the wider developer community. She is also the co-author of a book on the collaboration between artificial intelligence and neuroscience, reflecting her interest in how human cognition and intelligent systems can inform each other.

Through datactik, writing, and community involvement, Alexia helps organizations bridge the gap between data science research and real-world applications.
