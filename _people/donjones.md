Don Jones is a Microsoft MVP with years of experience as a successful IT trainer. He has built a reputation for engaging, practical teaching that turns complicated technology concepts into skills people can actually use on the job.

He is the author of Own Your Tech Career, a book that helps technology professionals take ownership of their own development, from building skills to navigating the path ahead. Through his training, writing, and speaking, Don focuses on helping people build durable, self-directed careers in technology.
