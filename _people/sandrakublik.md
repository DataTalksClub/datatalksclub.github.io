Sandra Kublik has spent her career turning AI enthusiasm into working businesses and communities. As an AI entrepreneur and evangelist, she has served as a mentor and coach to AI-first companies, co-founded the world's first independent AI acceleration program for startups, and grown and scaled a global hackathon community of AI professionals and enthusiasts.

She was part of the early GPT community, with involvement in initiatives such as Nextgrid and Lablab.ai, and remains an active spokeswoman on NLP and synthetic media. On her YouTube channel she interviews ecosystem stakeholders and discusses groundbreaking AI trends with content that aims to be both fun and educational.

Sandra is the co-author, with Shubham Saboo, of GPT-3: Building Innovative NLP Products Using Large Language Models, a book that shows developers and entrepreneurs how to build real products on large language models. The book was featured as a DataTalks.Club Book of the Week.
