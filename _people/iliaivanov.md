Ilia Ivanov is a data scientist at OLX Europe, the online marketplace, where he has spent four years working in data science. His focus is on recommendations and NLP, two areas that sit at the core of any marketplace: helping users discover relevant listings and making sense of the text they generate. He builds and improves the models behind those everyday experiences.
