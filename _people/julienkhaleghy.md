Julien Khaleghy is the founder and CEO of SerpApi, the Austin-based company behind a real-time API for search engine results. He grew up in Paris and moved to Austin, Texas, where he founded SerpApi in December 2017.

The idea came from his own work: while building iOS apps that recognized dog breeds and food with machine learning, he needed large image datasets from Google Images and found scraping and parsing them extremely hard. SerpApi now handles proxies, CAPTCHAs and HTML parsing so developers get structured JSON from Google, Bing, Amazon, YouTube and other engines, used for SEO, AI and machine learning data, RAG and news monitoring.

Julien speaks about why search data infrastructure matters for trustworthy AI and why public information should stay accessible. He is a member of the Forbes Technology Council.
