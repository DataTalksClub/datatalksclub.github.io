Edoardo Dusi is a Senior Developer Advocate at AWS, based in Italy. He started as a software developer, moved into leading engineering teams, and then into developer relations, where he ran the DevRel & Community team at Storyblok before joining AWS.

He shares what he learns through talks, his blog and Buongiorno da Edo, a tech and AI podcast on YouTube that he writes and hosts himself.
