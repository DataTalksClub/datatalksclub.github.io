Harish Shenoy is a Solution Architect for Data & AI at Databricks with 16 years of experience in enterprise data and AI infrastructure. He spent 7 years at AWS as a Senior Technical Account Manager and Solutions Architect.

He led the launch of Amazon Aurora Limitless Database at re:Invent 2023, a top-4 S-team priority for Amazon. Aurora has grown into one of AWS's most widely adopted cloud-native databases, running at scale for thousands of enterprises globally.

He also published the first cloud-native database CI/CD architecture on the AWS Open Source Blog, a methodology using Liquibase and Jenkins that has been adopted by over 8,000 organizations worldwide and endorsed by Paul Duvall, author of the canonical textbook on Continuous Integration. He now focuses on database branching and lakebase architectures at Databricks.
